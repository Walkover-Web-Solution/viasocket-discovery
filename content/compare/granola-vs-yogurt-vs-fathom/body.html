Granola vs Yogurt vs Fathom: what they're like to use

These three differ on where your meeting data goes. Granola and Fathom are cloud products with free plans and paid tiers. Yogurt keeps everything on your machine.

Granola

Granola captures device audio without a bot and turns your own notes into a summary. The free Basic plan is a real trial, while the history limit and integrations push working teams to Business.

What I liked

  • Bot-free capture on desktop, iOS, Android and Apple Watch.
  • Custom note templates and AI chat across meetings.
  • Audio is deleted after transcription, per Granola.

What I didn't

  • Basic keeps only 30 days of meeting history.
  • Notion, Zapier and HubSpot links need Business.
  • SSO and HIPAA workspaces sit on the top Enterprise plan.

Best for: individuals and small teams who want clean notes without a bot in the call.

Yogurt

Yogurt is the privacy-first option: transcription and summaries run locally, and no bot joins the call. It is young, with about 1,900 GitHub stars, and it asks for a capable machine and a one-time model download.

What I liked

  • Audio, transcripts and AI processing stay on your computer.
  • Free, open source and usable without an account.
  • Optional local MCP server and Markdown export.

What I didn't

  • Needs 8 GB of memory and a one-time model download.
  • Speaker names from calls work on Mac only for now.
  • While MCP access is on, any app under your account can read.

Best for: privacy-minded individuals who want free, local notes.

Fathom

Fathom's free plan is the most generous of the three on volume, and it lets you choose bot capture or a bot-free mode. The bot-free mode is a Mac beta, and sales features such as Deal View and CRM sync sit on Business.

What I liked

  • Free plan with unlimited recordings and transcription.
  • Choice of bot capture or bot-free (beta, Mac).
  • API and MCP, plus Zapier and CRM integrations.

What I didn't

  • Bot-free capture is a beta feature for Mac.
  • Team plans need at least two users.
  • CRM field sync and Deal View start at Business.

Best for: teams that want free recording volume and sales-call tools as they grow.

Which to choose

  • Choose Granola if you want a polished, bot-free notepad and will pay $14 for history and integrations.
  • Choose Yogurt if privacy and zero cost matter more than polish, and your computer can run local models.
  • Choose Fathom if you want free unlimited recordings now and sales-call tools later.

Whichever you pick, tell the people on the call you are recording.

FAQ

What is a free alternative to Granola?

Yogurt is a free, MIT-licensed alternative that runs on your computer. Fathom also has a free plan with unlimited recordings and transcripts.

How much does Granola cost?

Granola Basic is $0, Business is $14 per user per month and Enterprise is $35 per user per month, per its pricing page.

Does Yogurt send my meetings to the cloud?

Its README says audio, transcripts and AI processing stay on your computer. It goes online for model downloads and update checks.

Do these tools join my call as a bot?

Granola and Yogurt do not. Fathom offers bot capture, and a bot-free mode in beta for Mac.

Which supports MCP?

All three. Granola offers MCP on every plan, Fathom lists MCP and an API, and Yogurt has a local read-only MCP server that is off by default.
