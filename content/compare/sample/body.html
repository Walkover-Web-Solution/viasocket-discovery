Overview

Each comparison is a folder under content/compare. The folder name is the page address. meta.json describes the page, body.html is the article, and llm.txt is the plain-text copy for agents.

Sections come from the h2 headings. On a wide screen they stay in the left column. On a narrow screen they move into the jump menu above the article.

The sample is intentionally short. It exists so the index, the article, the text route, and the author link can be reviewed before the first real comparison arrives.

Reading the page

The middle column is the article. The right column is a viaSocket card that fades in after you scroll. On a phone that card becomes a bar along the bottom, with a control to close it.

Authors are linked from the byline. The same pages are listed on that person's author page, next to the blogs they already have.

Search engines get a canonical address, a description, Open Graph tags, and structured data for the article, the breadcrumb, and any question folded into a details block.

FAQ

Is this a real comparison?

No. This folder only proves the route, the layout, and the structured data.

How does a new page go live?

Add a folder under content/compare and ship that folder. The routes pick it up on the next build.
